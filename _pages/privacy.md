# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 14, 2026*

AgentsPulse publishes research reviews and uses limited measurement services to understand how the site performs. This page explains what may be collected when you visit.

## Analytics

AgentsPulse uses Google Analytics to measure aggregate activity such as page views, referring pages, device type, approximate location, and engagement. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies, subject to your consent where required.

## Advertising

AgentsPulse may use Google AdSense to display advertising. When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses, device information, and page context to serve, measure, limit, or personalize ads, subject to applicable law and your consent choices.

Advertising is clearly separated from editorial content. Advertisers do not review or control AgentsPulse editorial conclusions.

## Consent choices

Where required, a consent message will allow visitors to accept, reject, or manage relevant advertising and analytics choices. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may receive a consent flow provided through a Google-certified consent management platform.

You can also manage advertising preferences through [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) and learn how Google uses data on partner sites at [Google's partner-sites policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## External links

Articles link to papers, repositories, and other third-party sites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and AgentsPulse is not responsible for their content or data handling.

## Data retention and requests

AgentsPulse does not directly sell personal information. Aggregated analytics and advertising records are retained according to the settings of the relevant service providers. For privacy questions or requests, email [agentspulsecontact@163.com](mailto:agentspulsecontact@163.com). Please include only the information needed to explain your request; do not send passwords or other sensitive credentials.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the site's measurement, advertising, or legal requirements change. The revision date above identifies the latest version.
